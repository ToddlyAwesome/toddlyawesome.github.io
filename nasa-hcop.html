01 — The Challenge

An agency-wide document that needed to inspire.

NASA's Human Capital Operating Plan guides strategic workforce initiatives across the entire agency. It had to communicate complex policy effectively — while feeling worthy of the institution producing it.

The challenge: transform a dense government document into something people would actually want to read.

The Brief

Vision meets workforce strategy.

My role was to bring this important document to life through compelling visual design — ensuring it communicates effectively while resonating emotionally with its audience of scientists, engineers, and administrators.

03 — Design Decisions

Bold, modern, and unmistakably NASA.

The cover embodies NASA's mission with a futuristic aesthetic — clean lines and a color palette drawn directly from their brand identity. It sets the tone before the reader turns a single page.

Template System

Consistency at scale.

I designed a comprehensive template system ensuring visual uniformity across every section — headers, footers, section dividers, data callouts. Every element built to be functionally clear and aesthetically precise, supporting a cohesive narrative from start to finish.

04
Final Deliverables

A document that informs and inspires.

The final HCOP combined thoughtful design with NASA's rich legacy of exploration. The cover, interior graphic system, and template suite were delivered as a complete production-ready package.

The result isn't just a plan — it's a communication artifact that asks readers to take NASA's workforce strategy seriously, and gives them every visual reason to do so.

Impact

Design in service of a mission bigger than any one person.

By combining purposeful design with NASA's legacy of exploration, the HCOP now functions as both a strategic document and a cultural artifact — informing decisions and inspiring the people who carry them out.
