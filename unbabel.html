01

01 — Event & Convention Design

Bold designs for
busy trade show floors.

Convention graphics compete with hundreds of other booths for attention. For Unbabel's presence at Cannes and other major industry events, I prioritized bold, scalable designs that communicated the value proposition instantly — from forty feet away.

Every surface — from booth backdrops to handheld collateral — needed to read the same story: Unbabel makes your brand speak every language, fluently.

Chapter 02

Marketing
Collateral

02

02 — Digital & Print

Clear calls to action.
Consistent brand voice.

For digital ads and marketing materials, I focused on creating eye-catching visuals with unambiguous calls to action. Each piece balanced Unbabel's polished brand identity with the urgency and specificity that marketing performance demands.

The work spanned channels — web banners, social graphics, printed handouts, and deck templates teams could own and evolve.
