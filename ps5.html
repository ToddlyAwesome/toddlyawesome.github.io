Case Study — UX Design / Prototyping

PS5 Dashboard
Redesign

Reimagining PlayStation's home experience — from lo-fi wireframes to a fully interactive prototype.

Scroll

The PS5 dashboard is powerful — but cluttered. This redesign challenged every assumption about how players navigate their console, starting from scratch with research and ending with a prototype that puts games first.

Client

PlayStation / Sony

Role

UX Designer / Visual Designer

Deliverables

Wireframes, Hi-fi, Prototype

Tools

Figma

01 — The Problem

A dashboard built around Sony.
Not the player.

The existing PS5 home screen buries games behind promotional content, Store ads, and PlayStation Plus upsells. Players who just want to play have to navigate noise before reaching what they came for.

The core question: what if the dashboard adapted to the player — surfacing their games, their friends, their activity — instead of Sony's marketing agenda?

02 — Research & Wireframing

Start with structure,
not aesthetics.

Before touching color or style, the focus was on information architecture. Lo-fi wireframes mapped out every screen — Home, Mini Menu, Store, and item detail — with a clear hierarchy that puts games at the top.

01
Game-first home layout

Hero game area above the fold, quick-launch tiles prominent, store content subordinate.

02
Streamlined mini menu

Consolidated navigation with friends, notifications, search and settings in a single overlay — no buried submenus.

03
Cleaner store browsing

Grid-first store layout with a dedicated item detail page that surfaces buy, stream, and description without scroll overload.

03 — Visual Design

The PS5 language,
elevated.

The hi-fi designs kept PlayStation's visual DNA — deep blues, bold game art, the iconic controller button symbols — but reorganized everything with stronger hierarchy and breathing room.

Game art becomes the wallpaper. The mini menu becomes a focused overlay. The store becomes a gallery. Every screen communicates intent at a glance.

04 — Interactive Prototype

Try it yourself.

The fully interactive Figma prototype lets you navigate the redesigned home, mini menu, and store — exactly as a player would on the console.

figma.com — PS5 Dashboard Redesign

05 — Takeaway

A console dashboard should feel like yours — not a storefront you have to navigate around to get to your games.
