Chapter 02

SharePoint Design

02

02, SharePoint with ShortPoint

An intranet that actually
looks like the brand.

I designed and customized Samsung's internal SharePoint site using ShortPoint, creating a visually engaging, user-friendly platform that employees actually wanted to use. Layouts, navigation structures, and interactive components were all built from scratch to align with Samsung's branding.

Collaborating with internal stakeholders ensured the platform met organizational needs while maintaining a consistent, polished visual identity across every section.

03, Collaboration

Design that bridges
teams and goals.

Working at Samsung meant partnering with teams across departments, gathering requirements, incorporating feedback, and ensuring designs met the needs of diverse audiences ranging from engineers to executives.

I regularly presented concepts and design iterations directly to stakeholders, demonstrating the measurable impact of thoughtful visual design on communication, engagement, and brand perception.

Takeaway

Working inside one of the world's largest tech companies taught me that great design isn't just about aesthetics, it's about empowering people to communicate clearly, consistently, and at scale.
