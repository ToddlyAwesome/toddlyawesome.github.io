Overview

UI, brand, and graphic design that moves internal work forward.

This role allowed me to leverage my expertise in UI design, brand development, and graphic design — delivering impactful solutions that supported Samsung's internal branding and communication goals. As a Visual Designer at Samsung, I was responsible for enhancing the company's internal communication and branding through a wide range of design projects.

01 · Graphic Design Projects

Assets, templates, and identity — built to scale across teams.

01

A variety of design assets

Delivered a variety of graphic design assets — logos, documents, and presentations — tailored to support Samsung's internal initiatives and campaigns.

02

Brand consistency at scale

Maintained brand consistency across all materials, adhering to Samsung's global design guidelines so every asset felt like it belonged to the same company.

03

Templates teams could actually use

Created polished templates for reports and presentations, enabling teams to produce professional-looking deliverables efficiently — without needing a designer in the room every time.

Selected Work

02 · SharePoint Design with ShortPoint

An intranet that actually felt designed.

01

A platform worth visiting

Designed and customized Samsung's internal SharePoint site using ShortPoint, creating a visually engaging and user-friendly platform that teams wanted to use.

02

Layouts, nav, and interaction

Developed layouts, navigation structures, and interactive components to improve the site's functionality and align it with Samsung's branding — clarity over clutter, every time.

03

Designed with the team, not at them

Collaborated with internal stakeholders to ensure the site met organizational needs while maintaining a consistent visual identity across the experience.

03 · Cross-Functional Collaboration

Design happens with people, not at them.

The design work above didn't happen in a vacuum — it came out of constant back-and-forth with the teams who would actually use and own it.

01
Partnered with teams across departments to gather requirements and incorporate feedback, ensuring designs met the needs of diverse audiences.
02
Presented concepts and design iterations to stakeholders, demonstrating the impact of thoughtful visual design on communication and engagement.
03
Shipped design systems and templates that outlived the project — internal teams continued to build on the work well after handoff.
